# Hello 👋
I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Colorado State University, working with [Jim Hurrell](https://www.atmos.colostate.edu/people/faculty/hurrell/) and [Brooke Anderson](https://vetmedbiosci.colostate.edu/directory/member/?id=brooke-anderson-35248). My research focuses on the human health impacts of climate intervention, particularly Stratospheric Aerosol Injection (SAI). My Ph.D. examined stratospheric aerosols and their impacts - from evaluating the UK Earth System Model against a recent volcanic eruption, to assessing the climate impacts of multiple SAI strategies. I am now broadening into health research, combining climate science with epidemiological methods to understand how SAI could affect air quality and human health worldwide.

## 💭 My current projects
- 🏥 Developing an open-access workflow to calculate mortality due to surface ozone and PM2.5
- 🥵 Adding temperature related mortality to this workflow
- 🫁 Air quality mortality impacts under different SAI strategies
